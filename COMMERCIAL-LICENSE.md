# Commercial Licensing / 商业授权

EmbedPluginUI uses a dual-license model:

1. `LICENSE` — public non-commercial source license;
2. a separate written commercial license — required **before Commercial Use begins**.

This file explains the commercial licensing policy. It is not itself a commercial license grant.

## English

### When is a commercial license required?

A commercial license is required before use that directly or indirectly supports commercial advantage, paid delivery, for-profit production, or a commercial product. Typical examples include:

- firmware shipped in hardware that is sold, leased, licensed, or otherwise provided for compensation;
- robots, drones, vehicles, industrial equipment, consumer electronics, instruments, or other commercial devices containing EmbedPluginUI;
- paid software, SDKs, services, or subscriptions incorporating EmbedPluginUI or a modification of it;
- paid client deliverables;
- OEM/ODM or volume hardware deployments;
- internal production/business use by a for-profit company;
- any other use primarily directed toward commercial advantage or monetary compensation.

### Prior authorization is mandatory

The written commercial license must be obtained **before Commercial Use begins**.

A later purchase does not automatically retroactively authorize earlier unlicensed Commercial Use. Retroactive authorization, waiver, settlement, or release exists only if the Licensor expressly grants it in a separate written agreement.
